# Riding in Cars with Boys: recording evidence

Catalogue #935 has six numbered MP3s. Local ffprobe durations in order are 67.55, 67.85, 62.59, 67.04, 66.47 and 44.43 minutes (375.93 minutes total). Each file identifies Beverly Donofrio, an unabridged 06:16 album and reader Christina Moore. The first five files total approximately 331.50 minutes; this is close to the 330:42 runtime of the [OverDrive release](https://livebrary.overdrive.com/media/3320730) and 5h31m on the [Blackstone publisher listing](https://www.blackstonelibrary.com/riding-in-cars-with-boys). The [Apple Books product](https://books.apple.com/us/audiobook/riding-in-cars-with-boys-confessions-of-a-bad/id1640913215/) names the same author, narrator and Recorded Books publisher but lists 6h15m, close to all six local files. These products may share upstream data; they should not automatically be counted as independent recordings.

The close agreement between the five-file length and shorter release, and between the sixth file's length and the runtime discrepancy, is a clue that the last file could be additional content. It does not establish that: different timings, missing material, duplication, metadata errors, and a separate performance remain possible. A [published copyright-registration transcription](https://www.copyrightencyclopedia.com/cam-jansen-and-the-mystery-of-the-stolen-diamonds-alice/) describes a 2001 unabridged five-cassette recording with an author interview, but does not establish the interview's duration or whether it is in the local files. This is a secondary transcription, not the official registration record.

Next: compare the actual audio around the end of file five and start/end of file six with a legitimate sample or opening/closing credits; check for repeated content, an interview, and changes in narration. Do not silently discard file six, collapse the two durations into one, or approve processing solely from matching titles, narrator names and aggregate duration. Preserve both product assertions and their source-specific runtimes for calibration.

## Local names and accompanying description

The six filenames say only `CD 01 of 06` through `CD 06 of 06`; every embedded title is the book title and ffprobe reports no embedded chapter markers. They do not label an interview or bonus track. A neighbouring `info.txt` describes a six-CD Clipper Audio edition (ISBN 9781845050283, January 2004) with a 5h30m duration, despite the six local files totalling 6h15m56s. This is an additional local assertion, not independent proof that the files came from that edition. It weakens the hypothesis that five files are necessarily the main book and the sixth is an extra: the six-CD description itself gives the shorter duration. The near-equal five-file total could be coincidence or reflect differing transfers or contents. File five must not be treated as a confirmed boundary.

For other books, explicit labels such as interview, afterword or bonus in filenames, embedded chapter titles or sidecars are useful evidence for a candidate core/extra boundary. Preserve the original names and timestamps, distinguish ambiguous labels such as epilogue from optional material, and compare core and total durations separately with each release. A label or duration agreement should trigger review rather than automatically remove audio or prove a recording match.
