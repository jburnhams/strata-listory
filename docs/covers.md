# Covers

A stage between Listens and Processing: for every confirmed or accepted book or radio listen, find its cover at every source that knows the identified book and audiobook editions, keep the pictures at the highest size on offer, and let a person choose the preferred one. The choice is saved as the listen's `cover` choice (`listen_choice`), ready for the next stage.

## The job (`./app covers`, or "Search for covers" on the Covers page)
- **Automatic:** the server sweeps every `APP_COVER_SWEEP_SECONDS` (60; 0 turns it off) and queues a search for each listen with an accepted work (the model's acceptance or a person's), no chosen cover and no cover search yet (`run.unsearched`, one query), so a match accepted by any path is searched without a button. A person's approval on the match queue also queues it at once. A listen that changed since its search is left to the button or `./app covers --queue`.
- **Back to matches:** the card's button takes the accepted match back (`linkage.apply.reopen`: the work is a candidate again, candidates dropped by the approval are linked again, the listen is `review`) and the listen leaves this page until it is accepted again; the earlier answer stays in the log but is no longer a label.
- **Queued:** the page's button and `./app covers --queue` put one `covers`/`search` job per listen on the durable job queue (the same one as processing: one at a time with the other jobs, own process at low priority, restarts and timeouts handled, failures shown with Retry). A listen already queued is not queued twice; the job locks `listen:<id>`. `./app covers` without `--queue` still searches in the foreground.
- **Which listens:** kind `book`, `radio_series`, `radio_show` or `play`, a work accepted or confirmed, no active `cover` choice, and no finished cover search newer than the listen's newest change (`covers.run.pending`). Choosing a cover is not a change. `--force` or explicit ids search again.
- **What is known:** the accepted work's identifiers (all Audible ASINs, ISBNs, Open Library works, BBC programme ids and Wikidata items for a radio programme) plus the folder and files the listen was made from (`covers.run.context`).
- **Sources** (`covers/sources.py`; each returns pictures and a status, never raises): `audible` (product image of every ASIN from the local archive, size suffix stripped), `audnexus`, `openlibrary` (by ISBN, and the covers listed on the work and its editions, large), `apple` (title-matched, asked at 3000 px), `google` (by ISBN, largest link), `local` (an image in the folder, art embedded in the first audio file). For radio listens: `bbc` (`/programmes/<pid>.json` names the programme's image; asked as `ichef.bbci.co.uk/images/ic/raw/<image pid>.jpg` and as the 1920x1080 rendition, the larger that downloads standing for both; a radio listen with no programme id gets the ids of the radio index's programmes with exactly its title), `podcast` (Apple Podcasts artwork of a title match, 3000 px) and `wikidata` (the programme's P18 image or P154 logo through Commons `Special:FilePath`, 2000 px wide). Not used: Hardcover (needs a token), AudiobookDB (its terms exclude its images). Adding a source is one function and a line in `SOURCES`.
- **Padding:** a picture whose edges are one plain colour down two opposite sides (a book centred on a square canvas, or letterboxed) is stored without the bands (`imaging.padding` finds them on a 256x256 copy, `trim_padding` cuts the original with ffmpeg and keeps the format). Only a centred canvas counts: all four corners one colour, one axis padded, the two bands of similar width, at least 3% of the side and at most 60%; art that is plain all round (a white cover, a logo) is left alone. The cropped picture is what is hashed, sized and ranked, so a cover offered cropped by one source and padded by another is one picture and the square-shape signal no longer rewards the canvas. Pictures stored before this are not changed in place: searching the listen again reads each known address's stored picture back through the same step (`run.retrimmed`), so a repeat search gives the trimmed one.
- **Storage:** pictures once by sha256 under `<data_dir>/covers/` (`cover_image`: size, bytes, a 64-bit difference hash from ffmpeg); `cover_run` per search (every source's status and reason); `cover_candidate` per picture a source offered. Near-duplicates (equal or close hash, same shape) are shown as one cover with every source and the largest standing for it.
- **Politeness:** one request every 0.4 s per host, https only, 40 MB cap. A 429 is retried up to four times, waiting as long as `Retry-After` says (at most 60 s); a host that still says 429 is left alone for ten minutes and the source records `rate limited` (the search can be repeated from the page). Google Books allows few anonymous requests: an API key in `GOOGLE_BOOKS_API_KEY` or `~/.config/strata-listory/googlebooks.key` raises that.

## Best fit (`covers/fit.py`)
The pictures of a listen are ranked the way identification and the kind classifier decide: every piece of evidence is a *signal* with a hand-set weight, a picture's weights add to its score, and a softmax over the listen's pictures gives each a share ("how likely this is the one you would pick"). Signals: `audiobook cover` (+2.0: Audible, Audnexus or Apple Books audiobook) or `print edition only` (-0.5: Open Library or Google Books); `broadcaster's picture` (+2.5, BBC), `podcast artwork` (+1.0), `reference image` (+0.5, Wikidata); `from the listen's files` (+1.0); `this recording` (+1.5: the ASIN is the closest recording the identification found); `recording runtime` (+1.0 within 4% of the listen's length, -0.7 over 15% away); `title` (+0.5 filed under the book's title, -0.8 under another); `resolution` (0.8 x log2 of the longest side over 1000 px, between -2 and +1.6); `audiobook shape` (+0.8, square, books only); `agreement` (0.6 x log2 of the number of sources serving the same picture). A tie goes to the larger picture. The score and the signals are in the API and on the page ("Why this fit").

**Learning.** The hand-set weights are the prior; each signal has a multiplier (start 1, range 0-3) fitted as a conditional logit on the covers a person chose (the chosen picture against the same listen's others), regularised toward 1 (`PRIOR_STRENGTH` 3), so with no choices the ranking is the hand-set one and a signal moves only when choices show it. Accepting the suggestion counts half (it may only mean trust), choosing another picture counts fully. The weights are `data/covers/fit-model.json`; the page refits in the background after each choice and the ranking is computed when a page is read, so it applies at once; `./app covers --learn` refits by hand. A signal whose weight moved says so ("learned from your choices (was +2.0)").

## The page
Tabs: To choose, Chosen, None found, All searched; search by title. A card opens on the **best fit already selected**: its picture, every source that offered it (source, reference, size, bytes), why it ranks first, an **Accept** button (saves it) and, when there are others, **Expand N other choices**. Expanded, every choice is listed best fit first with its sources and reasons; clicking one highlights it, **Save** keeps the highlighted one, **Collapse** goes back. A saved card shows the saved cover with Clear choice, and Expand to change it. Any picture can be magnified (the magnify button, strata-react's `Lightbox`: zoom, pan, arrow keys through the choices, the sources beside it). Search again and what each source said (found / nothing / rate limited / failed / not tried, with the reason) as before.
